# IoT-Based Smart Laundry System Research Paper

I'm excited to share that my research paper on **IoT-based smart laundry identification systems using color sensors** has been published in the International Journal of Research Trends and Innovation (IJRTI)!

## About the Research

This paper explores an innovative approach to automating laundry sorting and identification using color sensor technology integrated with IoT systems. The research demonstrates how simple yet effective hardware solutions can solve everyday problems in smart home environments.

### Key Highlights

- **Focus**: Smart laundry identification using color sensor technology
- **Application**: Automated sorting and identification systems
- **Technology**: IoT hardware integration with color detection sensors
- **Publication**: IJRTI (International Journal of Research Trends and Innovation)
- **Date**: April 2024

## What the Paper Covers

The research presents a practical implementation of color sensor technology to identify and sort laundry items automatically. This type of automation can be particularly useful in:

- Smart home environments
- Commercial laundry facilities
- Automated sorting systems
- IoT-enabled household appliances

## Technical Approach

The system leverages:
- Color sensor hardware for fabric identification
- IoT connectivity for real-time data transmission
- Automated decision-making algorithms
- Integration with smart home ecosystems
